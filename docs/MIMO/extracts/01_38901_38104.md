## A. TR 38.901 §7.1 座標系統

### [901-001] 球座標定義（zenith / azimuth）
- 出處：TR 38.901 V19.4.0 §7.1.1, Figure 7.1.1, p.18
- 原文：> "Note that θ = 0 points to the zenith and θ = 90⁰ points to the horizon. The field component in the direction of θ̂ is given by F_θ and the field component in the direction of φ̂ is given by F_φ."
- 數值/型別/範圍：θ（zenith）量測自 +z 軸；φ（azimuth）在 x-y 平面內量測自 +x 軸（見 §7.1.3 原文 "θ is the zenith angle measured from the +z-axis, and φ is the azimuth angle measured from the +x-axis in the x-y plane"，p.21）。
- 說明：所有 field pattern 以 (F_θ, F_φ) 兩個分量表示。

### [901-002] GCS 與 LCS
- 出處：TR 38.901 V19.4.0 §7.1.2, p.19
- 原文：> "A Global Coordinate System (GCS) is defined for a system comprising multiple BSs and UTs. An array antenna for a BS or a UT can be defined in a Local Coordinate System (LCS). An LCS is used as a reference to define the vector far-field that is pattern and polarization, of each antenna element in an array. ... The placement of an array within the GCS is defined by the translation between the GCS and a LCS. The orientation of the array with respect to the GCS is defined in general by a sequence of rotations (described in clause 7.1.3)."
- 說明：element pattern 定義在 LCS；陣列在 GCS 中的位置 = 平移，朝向 = 旋轉。

### [901-003] 方位角 α / 下傾角 β / 斜角 γ 的定義
- 出處：TR 38.901 V19.4.0 §7.1.3, Figure 7.1.3-1/7.1.3-2, p.19–20
- 原文：> "The angle α is called the bearing angle, β is called the downtilt angle and γ is called the slant angle."
  > "a series of rotations about the z, ẏ and ẍ axes are assumed here, in that order. ... A first rotation of α about z sets the antenna bearing angle (i.e. the sector pointing direction for a BS antenna element). The second rotation of β about ẏ sets the antenna downtilt angle. Finally, the third rotation of γ about ẍ sets the antenna slant angle."
- 數值/型別：三個實數角度；intrinsic 旋轉順序 z → ẏ → ẍ。
- 說明：陣列朝向 = (α, β, γ)；§7.5 Step 1e 將 BS 朝向記為 Ω_BS,α/β/γ、UT 朝向記為 Ω_UT,α/β/γ（p.44）。

### [901-004] 複合旋轉矩陣
- 出處：TR 38.901 V19.4.0 §7.1.3, Eq. (7.1-2)–(7.1-5), p.20–21
- 原文（公式）：
  $$R = R_Z(\alpha)R_Y(\beta)R_X(\gamma)=\begin{bmatrix}\cos\alpha&-\sin\alpha&0\\ \sin\alpha&\cos\alpha&0\\0&0&1\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}\cos\beta&0&\sin\beta\\0&1&0\\-\sin\beta&0&\cos\beta\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}1&0&0\\0&\cos\gamma&-\sin\gamma\\0&\sin\gamma&\cos\gamma\end{bmatrix}\quad(7.1\text{-}2)$$
  $$R^{-1}=R_X(-\gamma)R_Y(-\beta)R_Z(-\alpha)=R^T\quad(7.1\text{-}3)$$
  $$R=\begin{bmatrix}\cos\alpha\cos\beta & \cos\alpha\sin\beta\sin\gamma-\sin\alpha\cos\gamma & \cos\alpha\sin\beta\cos\gamma+\sin\alpha\sin\gamma\\ \sin\alpha\cos\beta & \sin\alpha\sin\beta\sin\gamma+\cos\alpha\cos\gamma & \sin\alpha\sin\beta\cos\gamma-\cos\alpha\sin\gamma\\ -\sin\beta & \cos\beta\sin\gamma & \cos\beta\cos\gamma\end{bmatrix}\quad(7.1\text{-}4)$$
  （7.1-5 為其轉置。）原文："The reverse transformation is given by the inverse of R, which is also equal to the transpose of R since it is orthogonal."
- 說明：R 將 LCS 向量轉到 GCS；R⁻¹ 將 GCS 方向轉到 LCS。

### [901-005] GCS 角 → LCS 角
- 出處：TR 38.901 V19.4.0 §7.1.3, Eq. (7.1-6)–(7.1-8), p.21
- 原文：> "If this point represents a location in the GCS defined by θ and φ, the corresponding position in the LCS is given by R⁻¹ρ̂, from which local angles θ' and φ' can be computed."
  $$\hat\rho=[\sin\theta\cos\phi,\ \sin\theta\sin\phi,\ \cos\theta]^T\quad(7.1\text{-}6)$$
  $$\theta'=\arccos\!\big(\cos\beta\cos\gamma\cos\theta+(\sin\beta\cos\gamma\cos(\phi-\alpha)-\sin\gamma\sin(\phi-\alpha))\sin\theta\big)\quad(7.1\text{-}7)$$
  $$\phi'=\arg\!\big(\cos\beta\sin\theta\cos(\phi-\alpha)-\sin\beta\cos\theta+j(\cos\beta\sin\gamma\cos\theta+(\sin\beta\sin\gamma\cos(\phi-\alpha)+\cos\gamma\sin(\phi-\alpha))\sin\theta)\big)\quad(7.1\text{-}8)$$
- 說明：計算 element pattern 前先把 GCS 射線方向轉進 LCS。

### [901-006] Pattern 與 field 分量的 LCS→GCS 轉換
- 出處：TR 38.901 V19.4.0 §7.1.3, Eq. (7.1-1), (7.1-9)–(7.1-17), p.20–23
- 原文：> "Let A'(θ',φ') denote an antenna element pattern in the LCS and A(θ,φ) denote the same antenna element pattern in the GCS. Then the two are related simply by A(θ,φ) = A'(θ',φ')" (7.1-1)
  $$\begin{bmatrix}F_\theta(\theta,\phi)\\F_\phi(\theta,\phi)\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}\cos\psi&-\sin\psi\\ \sin\psi&\cos\psi\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}F'_{\theta'}(\theta',\phi')\\F'_{\phi'}(\theta',\phi')\end{bmatrix}\quad(7.1\text{-}11)$$
  $$\psi=\arg\big(\hat\theta(\theta,\phi)^T R\hat\theta'(\theta',\phi')+j\,\hat\phi(\theta,\phi)^T R\hat\theta'(\theta',\phi')\big)\quad(7.1\text{-}12)$$
  $$\hat\theta=[\cos\theta\cos\phi,\cos\theta\sin\phi,-\sin\theta]^T\ (7.1\text{-}13),\qquad \hat\phi=[-\sin\phi,\cos\phi,0]^T\ (7.1\text{-}14)$$
  $$\psi=\arg\big((\sin\gamma\cos\theta\sin(\phi-\alpha)+\cos\gamma(\cos\beta\sin\theta-\sin\beta\cos\theta\cos(\phi-\alpha)))+j(\sin\gamma\cos(\phi-\alpha)+\sin\beta\cos\gamma\sin(\phi-\alpha))\big)\quad(7.1\text{-}15)$$
  $$\cos\psi=\frac{\cos\beta\cos\gamma\sin\theta-(\sin\beta\cos\gamma\cos(\phi-\alpha)-\sin\gamma\sin(\phi-\alpha))\cos\theta}{\sqrt{1-(\cos\beta\cos\gamma\cos\theta+(\sin\beta\cos\gamma\cos(\phi-\alpha)-\sin\gamma\sin(\phi-\alpha))\sin\theta)^2}}\quad(7.1\text{-}16)$$
  $$\sin\psi=\frac{\sin\beta\cos\gamma\sin(\phi-\alpha)+\sin\gamma\cos(\phi-\alpha)}{\sqrt{1-(\cos\beta\cos\gamma\cos\theta+(\sin\beta\cos\gamma\cos(\phi-\alpha)-\sin\gamma\sin(\phi-\alpha))\sin\theta)^2}}\quad(7.1\text{-}17)$$
- 說明：功率 pattern 只需換角度；極化 field 需再乘 ψ 旋轉。

### [901-007] 只有機械下傾時的簡化式
- 出處：TR 38.901 V19.4.0 §7.1.4, Eq. (7.1-18)–(7.1-20), Figure 7.1.4, p.23
- 原文：> "...assuming that the orientation of the LCS ... is such that the bearing angle α=0, the downtilt angle β is non-zero and the slant angle γ=0. ... Mechanical downtilt is modelled as a rotation of the LCS around the y-axis. For zero mechanical downtilt the LCS coincides with the GCS."
  $$\theta'=\arccos(\cos\phi\sin\theta\sin\beta+\cos\theta\cos\beta)\ (7.1\text{-}18),\quad \phi'=\arg(\cos\phi\sin\theta\cos\beta-\cos\theta\sin\beta+j\sin\phi\sin\theta)\ (7.1\text{-}19)$$
- 說明：β 為機械下傾；(7.1-7)(7.1-8) 在 α=γ=0 時化簡為此式。

## B. TR 38.901 §7.3 天線模型

### [901-010] BS 天線：均勻矩形 panel 陣列
- 出處：TR 38.901 V19.4.0 §7.3.0 "BS antenna model", p.28
- 原文：> "This clause captures the antenna array structures considered in this SI for calibration."
  > "The BS antenna is modelled by a uniform rectangular panel array, comprising M_gN_g panels, as illustrated in Figure 7.3-1 with M_g being the number of panels in a column and N_g being the number of panels in a row."
- 數值/型別：M_g、N_g 為正整數；panel 總數 = M_g·N_g。
- 說明：§7.3.0 原文第一句說明此結構是為 **calibration** 而列，不是硬體強制規格。

### [901-011] Panel 間距 d_g,H / d_g,V
- 出處：TR 38.901 V19.4.0 §7.3.0, p.28；Figure 7.3-1, p.29
- 原文：> "Antenna panels are uniformly spaced in the horizontal direction with a spacing of d_g,H and in the vertical direction with a spacing of d_g,V."
- 數值：calibration 表中以波長為單位，例如 d_H,g = d_V,g = 2.5λ（Table 7.8-2、7.8-3）或 4λ（Table 7.8-4）。

### [901-012] Panel 內 element 排列：M、N
- 出處：TR 38.901 V19.4.0 §7.3.0, p.28
- 原文：> "On each antenna panel, antenna elements are placed in the vertical and horizontal direction, where N is the number of columns, M is the number of antenna elements with the same polarization in each column."
- 數值/型別：M 是每一行（column）中**相同極化**的 element 數（垂直方向），N 是行數（水平方向）。
- 說明：雙極化時每個位置有 2 個 element，因此每 panel 的 element 總數為 M·N·P（此乘積為推導，原文未寫出）。

### [901-013] Element 編號規則
- 出處：TR 38.901 V19.4.0 §7.3.0, p.28；Figure 7.3-1, p.29
- 原文：> "Antenna numbering on the panel illustrated in Figure 7.3-1 assumes observation of the antenna array from the front (with x-axis pointing towards broad-side and increasing y-coordinate for increasing column number)."
- 數值：Figure 7.3-1 的 element 標記為 (row, column)：左下 (0,0)、右下 (0,N-1)、左上 (M-1,0)、右上 (M-1,N-1)，每個位置畫成紅藍交叉的雙極化 element。
- 說明：row 索引 0…M-1 由下往上，column 索引 0…N-1 往 +y 方向遞增。

### [901-014] Element 間距 d_H / d_V
- 出處：TR 38.901 V19.4.0 §7.3.0, p.28

### [901-030] 天線 port mapping：傳統 ULA 的垂直 tilt 權重
- 出處：TR 38.901 V19.4.0 §7.3.1, Eq. (7.3-1), p.32

### [901-032] 極化 Model-1

### [901-034] 手持 UT 的極化場型（1 個或 2 個 field pattern）
- 出處：TR 38.901 V19.4.0 §7.3.2 "Handheld UT Model", Eq. (7.3-6)–(7.3-8), p.33–34
- 原文：> "For cases when a candidate antenna placement location is used for one antenna field pattern: Reference radiation pattern of the UT antenna model has the gain in the θ field component, F''_θ'' = √A'' and F''_φ'' = 0."
  > "For cases when a candidate antenna placement location is used for two distinct antenna polarization field patterns: ... ## E. TS 38.104 V19.5.0 §3.1 定義

（頁碼：38.104 定義頁 p.20–24）

### [104-001] beam
- 出處：TS 38.104 V19.5.0 §3.1, p.20
- 原文：> "beam: beam (of the antenna) is the main lobe of the radiation pattern of an antenna array" ; NOTE: "For certain BS antenna array, there may be more than one beam."

### [104-002] beam centre direction
- 出處：§3.1, p.20
- 原文：> "beam centre direction: direction equal to the geometric centre of the half-power contour of the beam"

### [104-003] beam direction pair
- 出處：§3.1, p.20
- 原文：> "beam direction pair: data set consisting of the beam centre direction and the related beam peak direction"

### [104-004] beam peak direction
- 出處：§3.1, p.20
- 原文：> "beam peak direction: direction where the maximum EIRP is found"

### [104-005] beamwidth
- 出處：§3.1, p.20
- 原文：> "beamwidth: beam which has a half-power contour that is essentially elliptical, the half-power beamwidths in the two pattern cuts that respectively contain the major and minor axis of the ellipse"
- 說明：beamwidth 以兩個正交切面的半功率寬度表示。

### [104-006] BS type 1-C / 1-H / 1-O / 2-O
- 出處：§3.1, p.21
- 原文：
  > "BS type 1-C: NR base station operating at FR1 with requirements set consisting only of conducted requirements defined at individual antenna connectors"
  > "BS type 1-H: NR base station operating at FR1 with a requirement set consisting of conducted requirements defined at individual TAB connectors and OTA requirements defined at RIB"
  > "BS type 1-O: NR base station operating at FR1 with a requirement set consisting only of OTA requirements defined at the RIB"
  > "BS type 2-O: NR base station operating at FR2 with a requirement set consisting only of OTA requirements defined at the RIB"
- 型別：enum {1-C, 1-H, 1-O, 2-O}。

### [104-007] antenna connector / active transmitter unit
- 出處：§3.1, p.20
- 原文：> "antenna connector: connector at the conducted interface of the BS type 1-C"
  > "active transmitter unit: transmitter unit which is ON, and has the ability to send modulated data streams that are parallel and distinct to those sent from other transmitter units to a BS type 1-C antenna connector, or to one or more BS type 1-H TAB connectors at the transceiver array boundary"

### [104-008] directional requirement
- 出處：§3.1, p.21
- 原文：> "directional requirement: requirement which is applied in a specific direction within the OTA coverage range for the Tx and when the AoA of the incident wave of a received signal is within the OTA REFSENS RoAoA or the minSENS RoAoA as appropriate for the receiver"

### [104-009] EIRP / EIS
- 出處：§3.1, p.21
- 原文：> "equivalent isotropic radiated power: equivalent power radiated from an isotropic directivity device producing the same field intensity at a point of observation as the field intensity radiated in the direction of the same point of observation by the discussed device" ; NOTE: "Isotropic directivity is equal in all directions (i.e. 0 dBi)."
  > "equivalent isotropic sensitivity: sensitivity for an isotropic directivity device equivalent to the sensitivity of the discussed device exposed to an incoming wave from a defined AoA" ; NOTE 1: "The sensitivity is the minimum received power level at which specific requirement is met."
- 說明：§3.3 的縮寫表把 EIRP 寫成 "Effective Isotropic Radiated Power"（p.27），與 §3.1 用詞 "equivalent" 不一致，屬原文用詞差異。

### [104-010] fractional bandwidth
- 出處：§3.1, p.21
- 原文：> "fractional bandwidth: fractional bandwidth FBW is defined as FBW = 200 · (F_FBWhigh − F_FBWlow)/(F_FBWhigh + F_FBWlow) %"

### [104-011] maximum carrier/total TRP output power
- 出處：§3.1, p.22
- 原文：> "maximum carrier TRP output power: mean power level measured per RIB during the transmitter ON period for a specific carrier in a specified reference condition and corresponding to the declared rated carrier TRP output power (P_rated,c,TRP)"
  > "maximum total TRP output power: mean power level measured per RIB during the transmitter ON period in a specified reference condition and corresponding to the declared rated total TRP output power (P_rated,t,TRP)"

### [104-012] minSENS / minSENS RoAoA
- 出處：§3.1, p.22
- 原文：> "minSENS: the lowest declared EIS value for the OSDD's declared for OTA sensitivity requirement." ; "minSENS RoAoA: The reference RoAoA associated with the OSDD with the lowest declared EIS"

### [104-013] OTA coverage range
- 出處：§3.1, p.23
- 原文：> "OTA coverage range: a common range of directions within which TX OTA requirements that are neither specified in the OTA peak directions sets nor as TRP requirement are intended to be met"

### [104-014] OTA peak directions set
- 出處：§3.1, p.23
- 原文：> "OTA peak directions set: set(s) of beam peak directions within which certain TX OTA requirements are intended to be met, where all OTA peak directions set(s) are subsets of the OTA coverage range" ; NOTE: "The beam peak directions are related to a corresponding contiguous range or discrete list of beam centre directions by the beam direction pairs included in the set."
- 型別：集合；可以是連續範圍或離散清單；必須是 OTA coverage range 的子集。

### [104-015] OTA REFSENS RoAoA
- 出處：§3.1, p.23
- 原文：> "OTA REFSENS RoAoA: the RoAoA determined by the contour defined by the points at which the achieved EIS is 3dB higher than the achieved EIS in the reference direction assuming that for any AoA, the receiver gain is optimized for that AoA" ; NOTE: "This contour will be related to the average element/sub-array radiation pattern 3dB beamwidth."

### [104-016] OTA sensitivity directions declaration (OSDD)
- 出處：§3.1, p.23
- 原文：> "OTA sensitivity directions declaration: set of manufacturer declarations comprising at least one set of declared minimum EIS values (with BS channel bandwidth), and related directions over which the EIS applies" ; NOTE: "All the directions apply to all the EIS values in an OSDD."

### [104-017] polarization match
- 出處：§3.1, p.23
- 原文：> "polarization match: condition that exists when a plane wave, incident upon an antenna from a given direction, has a polarization that is the same as the receiving polarization of the antenna in that direction"

### [104-018] radiated interface boundary (RIB)
- 出處：§3.1, p.23
- 原文：> "radiated interface boundary: operating band specific radiated requirements reference where the radiated requirements apply" ; NOTE: "For requirements based on EIRP/EIS, the radiated interface boundary is associated to the far-field region"

### [104-019] rated beam EIRP
- 出處：§3.1, p.23
- 原文：> "rated beam EIRP: For a declared beam and beam direction pair, the rated beam EIRP level is the maximum power that the base station is declared to radiate at the associated beam peak direction during the transmitter ON period"

### [104-020] rated carrier/total (TRP) output power
- 出處：§3.1, p.23
- 原文：> "rated carrier output power: mean power level associated with a particular carrier the manufacturer has declared to be available at the indicated interface, during the transmitter ON period in a specified reference condition"
  > "rated carrier TRP output power: mean power level declared by the manufacturer per carrier, for BS operating in single carrier, multi-carrier, or carrier aggregation configurations that the manufacturer has declared to be available at the RIB during the transmitter ON period"
  > "rated total output power: mean power level associated with a particular operating band the manufacturer has declared to be available at the indicated interface ..." ; "rated total TRP output power: mean power level declared by the manufacturer, that the manufacturer has declared to be available at the RIB during the transmitter ON period"

### [104-021] reference beam direction pair
- 出處：§3.1, p.23
- 原文：> "reference beam direction pair: declared beam direction pair, including reference beam centre direction and reference beam peak direction where the reference beam peak direction is the direction for the intended maximum EIRP within the OTA peak directions set"

### [104-022] receiver target 系列 / reference RoAoA / sensitivity RoAoA
- 出處：§3.1, p.23–24
- 原文：> "receiver target: AoA in which reception is performed by BS types 1-H or BS type 1-O"
  > "receiver target redirection range: union of all the sensitivity RoAoA achievable through redirecting the receiver target related to particular OSDD"
  > "receiver target reference direction: direction inside the OTA sensitivity directions declaration declared by the manufacturer for conformance testing. For an OSDD without receiver target redirection range, this is a direction inside the sensitivity RoAoA"
  > "reference RoAoA: the sensitivity RoAoA associated with the receiver target reference direction for each OSDD."
  > "sensitivity RoAoA: RoAoA within the OTA sensitivity directions declaration, within which the declared EIS(s) of an OSDD is intended to be achieved at any instance of time for a specific BS direction setting"

### [104-023] requirement set
- 出處：§3.1, p.24
- 原文：> "requirement set: one of the NR base station requirement's set as defined for BS type 1-C, BS type 1-H, BS type 1-O, and BS type 2-O"

### [104-024] TAB connector 與 TX/RX min cell group
- 出處：§3.1, p.24
- 原文：> "TAB connector: transceiver array boundary connector"
  > "TAB connector RX min cell group: operating band specific declared group of TAB connectors to which BS type 1-H conducted RX requirements are applied"
  > "TAB connector TX min cell group: operating band specific declared group of TAB connectors to which BS type 1-H conducted TX requirements are applied." ; NOTE: "Within this definition, the group corresponds to the group of TAB connectors which are responsible for transmitting a cell when the BS type 1-H setting corresponding to the declared minimum number of cells with transmission on all TAB connectors supporting an operating band, but its existence is not limited to that condition"

### [104-025] total radiated power (TRP)
- 出處：§3.1, p.24
- 原文：> "total radiated power: is the total power radiated by the antenna" ; NOTE: "The total radiated power is the power radiating in all direction for two orthogonal polarizations. Total radiated power is defined in both the near-field region and the far-field region"

### [104-026] transceiver array boundary
- 出處：§3.1, p.24
- 原文：> "transceiver array boundary: conducted interface between the transceiver unit array and the composite antenna"

## G. TS 38.104：宣告（declaration）與功率規則

### [104-040] 波束宣告項目（radiated transmit power）
- 出處：TS 38.104 V19.5.0 §9.2.1, p.228
- 原文：

  > "For each beam, the requirement is based on declaration of a beam identity, reference beam direction pair, beamwidth, rated beam EIRP, OTA peak directions set, the beam direction pairs at the maximum steering directions and their associated rated beam EIRP and beamwidth(s)."
  > "For each beam peak direction associated with a beam direction pair within the OTA peak directions set, a specific rated beam EIRP level may be claimed. ## H. GPT 文件錯誤／不精確之處

參考文件：`GPT_search.md`（下稱 G）、`spec_v1.md`（下稱 S）。

1. **38.104 版號錯誤（S）**：S 第 130 行與第 947 行寫 "TS 38.104 V19.4.0"，本地檔案與引用應為 **V19.5.0**（ETSI TS 138 104 V19.5.0，2026-08）。G 使用 V19.5.0，正確。
2. **把 1-H 架構當成所有 BS 的架構（G §5、S Layer 2）**：TRXUA→TAB→RDN→AA 只適用 **BS type 1-H**（§4.3.2, Figure 4.3.2-1, p.30）。BS type 1-O/2-O 沒有 TAB（§4.3.3, Figure 4.3.3-1, p.31）；BS type 1-C 只有 antenna connector（§4.3.1, p.29）。
3. **遺漏 BS type 1-O 的最少單元數（G、S）**：「TX/RX 單元數 implementation specific」是 §4.3.2（1-H）的原文，正確；但 §4.3.3 規定 "For a BS type 1-O the transceiver unit array must contain at least 8 transmitter units and at least 8 receiver units"（p.31）。兩份文件都沒有提到。
4. **"38.104 沒有 element/TRX mapping" 的說法不完整（S 第 403–415、942 行；G）**：38.104 本身確實沒有，但 **TR 38.901 §7.8 的 calibration 表**有明文記法 (M, N, P, M_g, N_g; M_p, N_p)，並定義 "M_p and N_p are the number of vertical, horizontal TXRUs within a panel and polarization"（Table 7.8-1A p.101、7.8-2A p.103、7.8-6A p.108、7.8-6B p.110、7.8-6C p.111），以及 "The 10 elements are mapped to a single CRS port"（Table 7.8-1 p.100）等 port mapping。兩份文件都沒有提到這組官方的 element→port 維度。
5. **tuple 順序（G、S）**：寫 (M_g, N_g, M, N, P) 與 §7.3.0（p.28）一致，正確；但沒有指出 calibration 表使用 **(M, N, P, M_g, N_g; M_p, N_p)** 的不同順序，直接套用容易把欄位對錯。
6. **element pattern 參數名稱不精確（G 第 229–233 行）**：G 使用 "front_back_attenuation_dB: 30"、"side_lobe_attenuation_dB: 30"，這些名稱不在原文中。Table 7.3-1（p.29）的名稱是 **SLA_V = 30 dB**（垂直切面上限）與 **A_max = 30 dB**（水平切面與 3D pattern 的上限），另有 θ_3dB = 65°、φ_3dB = 65°、G_E,max = 8 dBi。數值正確。
7. **A_max 的說明不完整（S 第 72–74 行）**：S 寫 "maximum attenuation in 3D pattern"。原文中 A_max 同時是**水平切面**的上限與 3D pattern 的上限（Table 7.3-1, p.29）。
8. **沒有提到 UT pattern（G、S）**：手持 UT 使用另一組參數：θ_3dB = φ_3dB = 125°、SLA_V = A_max = 22.5 dB、G_E,max = 5.3 dBi（Table 7.3-2, p.32）。兩份文件只列 BS 的 65°/8 dBi。
9. **±45° 的來源（S 第 395 行）**：S 說 ±45° "是 §7.3.2 明文使用的 cross-polarized pair"，這句正確（Model-1 定義，p.33）。但 calibration 的實際設定是 "X-pol (+/-45 degree) ... for BS antenna configuration 1 and X-pol (0/+90 degree) ... for UT"（§7.8.2, p.101），而且 calibration 採用的是 **Model-2**（Table 7.8-2 "Model-2 in TR36.873"；Table 7.8-2A/6A/6B/6C "Model-2 in Clause 7.3.2"），不是 Model-1。
